The workshop

A few people who like building things.

BunkLyf isn't a company with a mission statement. It's a shared table where we build the tools we wished existed, argue about corner radius, and ship the ones that survive the arguing.

  • Small on purpose

    If a thing needs a tutorial, we built it wrong. Each app does one job and stays out of the way for the other twenty-three hours.

  • Nothing leaves your browser

    No accounts, no trackers, no analytics quietly following you around. What happens in your tabs is genuinely your business.

  • Fun is a feature

    A koala napping in the corner of your screen is not a serious design decision. It also makes people keep the extension installed. Both things are true.
